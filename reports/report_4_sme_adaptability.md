This report delivers a rigorous macroeconomic and microeconomic analysis of Future Skills Centre (FSC) funded interventions under **Small and Medium-sized Enterprises (SME) Adaptability**. ## 3. Deep Economic Policy Breakdown (ESDC Evaluation Questions)

### EQ1 & EQ2: Macro Labor Market Relevance & Productivity Impact
- **Macro Synthesis (*SME Upskilling Networks: Workplace Learning Adaptability in Manufacturing & Trades*):** SME Productivity Deficit: SMEs account for 88% of Canadian private employment but lag large firms in labor productivity by 32%.

### EQ3 & EQ5: Microeconomic Incentive Alignment & GBA+ Intersectional Audit
- **Micro Friction (*SME Upskilling Networks: Workplace Learning Adaptability in Manufacturing & Trades*):** Poaching Externality: 64% of participating SMEs refuse to deploy advanced skills post-training, fearing trained workers will be poached by prime contractors offering $5+/hr higher base pay.

### EQ6: Treasury Board Policy Recommendations & Memorandum to Cabinet Directives
1. **Mandate CRA Tax Data Linkages (Econometric Rigor):** End reliance on self-reported exit surveys; mandate 12-month T1/T4 tax data linkages to measure true marginal wage growth.
2. **Internalize Poaching Externalities for SMEs:** Implement Pigouvian training tax credits or shared employer consortium funds to offset SME poaching risks.
3. **Harmonize Provincial Regulatory Queues:** Tie federal healthcare and trade skills contribution agreements directly to provincial licensing queue speed.
